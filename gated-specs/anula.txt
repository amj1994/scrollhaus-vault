Build a luxury handcrafted jewelry brand landing page for "ANULA" — a single-page app using
Vite + Express with an index.html entry point served through Vite's SPA middleware in
development and Express static serving in production. Scroll-driven video scrubbing, a
horizontal product carousel with video reveal, a two-column stats section, and a fixed footer
reveal. GSAP + ScrollTrigger drives every scroll animation.

===============================================================================
BUILD & SERVING ARCHITECTURE
===============================================================================
- Dev: Express (tsx server.ts) with Vite mounted as middleware
  createViteServer({ server:{ middlewareMode:true }, appType:"spa" })
- Build: vite build -> dist/, then esbuild bundles server.ts -> dist/server.cjs
  (Node CJS, --packages=external)
- Production: Express serves dist/ static with a catch-all to index.html
- Page content lives in index.html at the project root with inline <style> and <script>
- Express exposes GET /api/higgsfield-video, resolving a Higgsfield share page to its direct
  MP4 (og:video meta first, bare .mp4 as fallback), returning { success, url }.
  IMPORTANT: expose it, but do NOT let the page call it to replace its own <video> sources.
  It resolves to whatever that share page points at — a third-party asset — and will silently
  overwrite your own hero on load. Self-host the clips from /assets.
- Pick a port that is actually free. 3000 is a common collision; if a smoke test returns HTML
  you did not write (Next.js response headers are the giveaway) something else owns it.

2. SCROLL-DRIVEN VIDEO HERO — .hero is height:500vh, background #000, z-index 10. Inside, a

   padding 48px). The <video> is absolute, object-fit:cover, z-index 1, playsinline muted
   preload="auto". Overlay content z-index 3, max-width 700px:
   - Title "WEIGHTED" / "IN LIGHT", Instrument Serif clamp(36px,6vw,72px), weight 400,
     line-height 1.05, letter-spacing -1px, #000. Each line is
     <span class="hero-title-line"><span class="hero-title-line-inner">...</span></span>

     border-radius 100px, padding 4px 4px 4px 18px, 13px/500 uppercase, plus a 28px #2e2e2e
     circle with a white + built from ::before (10x1.5px) and ::after (1.5x10px). Hover flips
     to black bg / white text / white circle with black cross. Click smooth-scrolls to footer.

   THE SCRUBBING TECHNIQUE — the single most important detail on the page:
     if (!video.seeking && Math.abs(video.currentTime - currentTime) > 0.01) {
       video.currentTime = currentTime;
     }
   The !video.seeking guard tells the browser to take a new frame ONLY once it has finished
   presenting the previous one. Without it the decoder is flooded with seeks and the picture
   freezes, blacks out and stutters. Combine with currentTime += (target - current) * 0.08
   inside a requestAnimationFrame loop. Use it on BOTH videos.
   Past 80% progress the hero characters individually fade, blur and shift up (cubic easing per
   character); the button fades with pow(progress,4).
   Split each .hero-title-line-inner into <span class="hero-char"> on load.

3. HORIZONTAL PRODUCT CAROUSEL — section 100vh, overflow hidden, 1px black top and bottom

===============================================================================
JS
===============================================================================
GSAP 3.12.5 + ScrollTrigger from cdn.jsdelivr.net. Behaviours: the scrub loop above; the
per-character hero exit; the pinned horizontal scroll plus video reveal; the staggered left panel
fade; the footer spacer sizing; debounced (200ms) resize that kills all ScrollTriggers and
rebuilds only when the width actually changed; and initialisation on document.fonts.ready rather
than window.load, so a slow video download never blocks the animations.

===============================================================================
ACCEPTANCE
===============================================================================
- Hero scrubs smoothly both directions with no black frames or stutter (the seeking guard).
- Hero copy exits per character past 80% progress and leaves no residue.
- Carousel pins, scrolls horizontally, then the reveal wrapper opens symmetrically to 100%.
- Left column is sticky through the stat column; stat cards show only the second h1.
- Footer reveals from behind the content; the spacer matches its height at every width.
- No third-party asset URLs anywhere in the built page — search for the original CDN prefix.
- Zero console errors; document.fonts.check passes for Instrument Serif and Inter Tight.
