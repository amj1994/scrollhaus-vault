Build a single-page website called "Pictura" (PIXEL PERFECT). Full-screen dark-mode landing page
(background #0E0E0E) where a photograph mosaic in the shape of a heart occupies the center of the
screen. As the user scrolls (mouse wheel or touch), the heart's large cards disperse to fixed final
positions around the viewport while a small pixel-heart in the center expands and reveals more
mini-photos. Clicking any dispersed card expands it into a centered modal with metadata. There is
no vertical page scroll — scroll events drive an animation progress value (0..1), not the document.

## 1. Stack
TanStack Start v1 (React 19, Vite 7). File-based routing under `src/routes/`. Tailwind CSS v4 via
`@import "tailwindcss"` in `src/styles.css`. No shadcn components on the page — everything is plain
divs/inline styles plus a single `<style>` block for keyframes. Fonts loaded through a
`<link rel="stylesheet">` in `__root.tsx` `head()` — Instrument Serif (regular + italic) and Inter
Tight (400, 500). `html`/`body`: background #0E0E0E, color #FCFCFC, overflow hidden, height 100%.

## 2. Remote assets — hotlinked, NOT downloaded
Every image and icon is referenced by its exact `https://qclay.design/lovable/pictura/...` URL and
is NOT self-hosted — the source spec explicitly forbids downloading them. 43 large heart photos
(person1..person44.png, skipping person8), 50 small heart photos (person_small/person1..50.png),
plus favicon/arrows/frame.svg and 9 modal icons, all under that same base path.

## 3. Layout hierarchy
1. A `<style>` block with all keyframes (logoDraw, fadeInUp, lineGrow, letterIn, wordIn, wordInDim,
   paraWordIn, arrowIn, btnPop, btnExpand, cellReveal, smallCubeIn, smallImgIn, imgFadeIn, modalFade,
   pillPop, tagIn) plus a `.modal-img-wrap::after` gradient rule and `.gs-hoverable` hover rules.
2. A decorative `frame.svg`, absolutely positioned, blurred 14px while a modal is open.
3. Header (z-30): an animated burger button (two lines, scaleX 0→1 on load, then scaleX 1↔0.53 on
   menu toggle), a centered 8-ray hand-drawn StarLogo + letter-by-letter "PICTURA" wordmark (final
   "A" italic), and an animated "Get Started" pill button (outline variant) on the right.
4. Sidebar menu (z-40): dim/blur overlay + a 340px-wide slide-in `<aside>` with nav links
   (Explore/Collections/Photographers/Pricing/About/Contact) in Instrument Serif 36px.
5. The heart container: 8 rows of large photo cards (4,6,6,7,8,6,4,2 = 43 cards) built from exact
   pixel dimensions, each revealed in serpentine per-row order with a blur+rise entrance. A
   `SmallHeart` sits inside row 3's notch — 11 rows of tiny 5.067px cells that pop in by Chebyshev
   distance from center, fade in their first photo in snake order, then continuously crossfade
   through a 50-image pool once scrolling starts, all timed by exact millisecond constants.
6. Bottom typography: dim-to-white word-reveal H1 "Find the photo" (left), a center paragraph +
   filled "Get Started" button, and a right-aligned H2 "that reflects your ideas" — all at
   93.554px Instrument Serif, fading out as scroll progress increases.
7. Card modal: clicking a dispersed card (only once progress ≥ 0.95) grows it from its exact
   clicked-element rect to a centered 481×638.716px card in 0.55s. Icon rail (copy/like/dislike) to
   its left; a white "Add to Collection" pill overlapping the top edge whose letters animate in
   REVERSE order (last letter first); title "Ludita Goyongo." + tick-circle + maximize icon; a tag
   row (Model/Aesthetic/Green/Photosession); a keyboard hint row 95px below. ESC closes,
   ArrowLeft/Right paginate through the dispersed cards, clicking the overlay closes.

## 4. Interaction/state model
A non-passive `wheel` listener (plus touch equivalents) accumulates deltaY into a 0..600 value and
derives `progress = accum/600`, calling `e.preventDefault()` so the document itself never scrolls.
Progress linearly interpolates every big card from its heart-grid position to an exact per-photo
`TARGETS` map entry (left/right/top/bottom or a raw `translate()`) — cards with no map entry instead
fly off-screen along a per-card deterministic scatter vector. The same progress value drives the
small heart's scale (1 → 2.223×), its cell background lerp (#FFF → #D9D9D9), and its "expand" image
layer's opacity. Once progress > 0, a 1200ms interval continuously cycles every small-heart cell to
the next image in a 50-image pool via a 600ms crossfade. After progress reaches 1, mouse movement
drives a per-card inverse-parallax effect (5–35px range, inversely proportional to card area, with
deterministic sine-based jitter), eased at 0.08/frame via requestAnimationFrame.

## 5. Exact numeric constants (do not round or "clean up")
Big heart row sizes: 126.962 / 126.962 (width 112.025) / 74.684 / 59.747 / 37.342 (×4 rows).
H_GAP = V_GAP = 10, with a 124.43px gap after row-0 column 1 and a 90px gap after row-2 column 2.
Small heart: 11 rows of 6/11/13/13/13/11/9/7/5/3/1 cells, 5.067px cell, 1px gap, 19.201px row-0
notch, positioned at left 322.139 / top 278.397 inside the big heart container. Modal: 481 ×
638.716px. Bottom H1/H2: 93.554px, letter-spacing -2.807px, line-height 90%.

## 6. Verification
On load: burger lines animate in, star logo draws its 8 rays, "PICTURA" letters blur in, the
outline "Get Started" button pops from a 41px circle to 150px wide with letters + arrow. Heart
cells reveal serpentine-per-row with blur+rise; the small heart pops in by Chebyshev distance, then
fades in its base photos in snake order. Scrolling disperses every targeted card to its exact
`TARGETS` position (untargeted cards fly off-screen); the small heart scales up, darkens, and its
"expand" layer fades in. Every 1.2s the small heart's cells crossfade to their next pool image.
Past progress 1, mouse movement parallaxes every big card. Clicking a dispersed card opens the
481×638.716 modal growing from its own rect; content staggers in on the documented delays.
