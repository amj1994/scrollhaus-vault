Build a one-page TanStack Start (React 19 + Vite + Tailwind v4) landing page for "Nixole" — AI
that converts patients for medical practices. Match the spec below pixel-for-pixel.

## 1. Stack
TanStack Start file-based routing (`src/routes/__root.tsx` + `src/routes/index.tsx`), Tailwind
v4 CSS-first config in `src/styles.css` (no tailwind.config.js), framer-motion for every
animation, Lenis for buttery inertia scrolling. Font: Inter Tight (300–700) via Google Fonts.
Title: "Nixole — AI that converts patients for your medical practice".

## 2. Design tokens
`src/styles.css` defines an oklch design-token theme (white background, near-black foreground,
a warm yellow accent `oklch(0.88 0.18 95)`), plus the `.mesh-showcase` animated radial-gradient
mesh background (7 layered radial gradients + a diagonal shimmer sweep + hue-rotate drift,
running continuously via `meshFlow`/`meshDrift`/`shimmerSweep` keyframes), a `.animate-marquee`
28s linear infinite scroll, and `.dot-pop` for the dashed-connector dots.

## 3. Layout
- **Header**: Nixole logo (mark + wordmark, letter-by-letter rise-in) on the left; center nav
  (Home/How it works/Company/Case Studies) fading up staggered; a "Book a demo" pill on the
  right, popping in with a back-out ease.
- **Hero**: an eyebrow pill ("AI" badge + "Autonomous calls, enrollment & payments"), then a
  three-line H1 built from per-word rise-in animations — "AI that converts patients" / "for
  your [inline circular looping video avatar] medical practice" (the muted-tone third line) —
  a subheading paragraph, and two CTA pills ("Book a demo" outlined, "Let's Talk" solid yellow).
  **The inline circular avatar is a real autoplaying/looping/muted video** — not a static
  photo — showing a warm, reassuring nurse portrait with barely-perceptible breathing/blink
  motion, generated fresh rather than reused stock footage.
- **Showcase card**: the full-width `.mesh-showcase` panel containing two inner cards — a dark
  "All-in-One Enrollment Platform" card (with a floating browser-mockup screenshot, a
  hand-drawn dashed-dot connector line animating in from the mockup to a "Key Features"
  glass popover listing 5 features with "Payments & Subscriptions" highlighted in yellow with
  a check), and a white testimonial card ("What our customers say", dated "Feb 02, 2026", a
  quote, an avatar+wordmark, and a 4.5-star rating). Below both cards, a "Trusted by…" line and
  an infinite-scrolling brand-logo marquee.

## 4. Assets
8 static files (logo mark, logo wordmark variant, browser mockup screenshot, avatar/wordmark
SVGs, feature-header arrow icon, Upside wordmark) are downloaded from the source CDN and
self-hosted under `src/assets/`, imported as modules (not hardcoded URLs) per the spec's own
instruction. The four "trusted by" brand marks (Intel, Oracle, GoFundMe, Nutanix) are rendered
as plain text wordmarks rather than their real trademarked logo artwork — the source spec
referenced that artwork from its own project file without inlining the actual path data, so it
wasn't available to copy verbatim.

## 5. Hero video generation
The circular nurse avatar clip was generated with Higgsfield: a still portrait (`soul_2`,
warm studio lighting, blue scrubs, reassuring expression) animated with `kling3_0` using the
same frame as both start and end reference, producing an extremely subtle breathing/blink loop
with no visible seam. Self-hosted as `hero-video.mp4`/`hero-video-poster.webp` — not a
downloaded stock clip.

## 6. Verification
Header logo + wordmark animate in letter-by-letter; eyebrow pill fades up; the H1 rises word by
word with the nurse video playing inline in a circle; both CTAs scale in with a back-out ease;
the mesh-showcase card rises into view with its gradient continuously animating; the dashed dot
line draws itself from the browser mockup to the Key Features popover; "Payments &
Subscriptions" stays highlighted yellow with a check icon; the testimonial card shows 4.5
yellow stars; the brand marquee loops seamlessly leftward forever.
